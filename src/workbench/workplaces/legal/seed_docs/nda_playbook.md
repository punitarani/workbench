# NDA Review Playbook — Customer Agreements

Maintained by the legal team. Applies to inbound and outbound NDAs signed in
connection with **customer** engagements. Last reviewed at the start of the
quarter.

## Standard positions

### 1. Definition of Confidential Information
Accept definitions covering information marked confidential or that a
reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Reject definitions
that sweep in all information disclosed, marked or not, without a
reasonableness qualifier.

### 2. Term
Customer NDAs: accept confidentiality obligations of three (3) years from
disclosure. Trade secrets survive as long as they remain trade secrets.

### 3. Residuals
Reject residuals clauses in all customer NDAs. No exceptions without GC
sign-off.

### 4. Non-solicitation
Customer NDAs occasionally attach hiring restrictions. Escalate to the GC;
do not accept silently.

### 5. Governing law
Accept Delaware, New York, or California. Escalate anything else.

### 6. Injunctive relief
Accept standard acknowledgments that breach may cause irreparable harm.
Reject clauses waiving the requirement to prove damages entirely.

## Process

1. Intake through the legal matter tracker; the paralegal opens a matter and
   assigns per the coverage rota.
2. Reviewing counsel marks each clause accept / negotiate / reject.
3. Redlines go back through the requesting business owner, not directly to
   the counterparty, unless counsel says otherwise.
4. Fully executed copies are filed in the repository under /legal/executed.

## Coverage notes

This playbook covers customer paper. For anything else, ask the commercial
team before taking a position.
