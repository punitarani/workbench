# Engagement Letter (Standard Form)

Calder & Finch, CPAs — Portland, Oregon

Dear [CLIENT],

Thank you for engaging Calder & Finch, CPAs. This letter confirms the
terms of our engagement and the nature and limitations of the services we
will provide.

## Scope of Services

We will perform the services described in Schedule A. Unless Schedule A
says otherwise, our work is limited to the periods and entities named
there. Services outside Schedule A — responding to notices, additional
filings, transaction support — are billed separately at our standard
rates after we confirm scope with you in writing.

## Your Responsibilities

You agree to provide complete and accurate information on a timely basis.
Our deadlines assume documents requested arrive within ten business days
of the request. We are not responsible for penalties or interest arising
from late or incomplete client-provided information.

## Fees

Fees are billed monthly as work progresses and are due on receipt.
Amounts unpaid after thirty days accrue a late charge of one percent per
month. We may suspend work on any engagement with an unpaid balance more
than sixty days old.

## Records

Your original records remain your property and will be returned on
request. Our workpapers are our property and are retained under our
records retention policy.

Please sign and return one copy of this letter. We appreciate the
opportunity to work with you.

Sincerely,

Calder & Finch, CPAs
