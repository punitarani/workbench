# Tax Return Workflow & Review Policy

Every return moves through the same five stations, in order. No station
may be skipped, including for extensions.

1. **Intake.** Engagement letter signed; prior-year file rolled forward;
   PBC request sent from the standard template. The preparer owns the
   open-items tracker from this point.
2. **Preparation.** Preparer completes the return and the supporting
   workpapers, clears software diagnostics, and self-reviews against the
   prior year before moving the file.
3. **Detail review.** A senior or manager reviews every schedule against
   support. Review notes are written in the file and must be cleared or
   answered in writing — verbal clearance does not count.
4. **Partner review & signature.** The signing partner reviews the
   return as a whole, the largest movements against prior year, and any
   position memos. Only a partner releases a return.
5. **Delivery & e-file.** The deliverable packet goes through the portal
   with the signature forms. E-filing happens only after the signed
   authorization is back. Acknowledgments are checked the next business
   day and filed.

## Extensions

An extension is a calculation, not a guess: current-year estimates must
be supported by a schedule in the file. The extension list is maintained
as one tracker and reviewed by the tax manager weekly from mid-March.

## Deadlines

Internal due dates run two weeks ahead of statutory dates. A return that
misses its internal date gets flagged at the weekly scheduling huddle.
