# Engagement Letter — Standard Form

**Hartwell & Marsh LLP** · 400 Harbor Plaza, Suite 1200, Oakland, CA

Dear [CLIENT CONTACT],

Thank you for selecting Hartwell & Marsh LLP to represent [CLIENT NAME] in
connection with [MATTER DESCRIPTION]. This letter confirms the terms of our
engagement.

## Scope of Representation

Our representation is limited to the matter described above. We are not
undertaking a general representation of the company, and we will not advise
on tax, securities, or regulatory questions unless separately engaged in
writing. Any expansion of scope requires a signed addendum.

## Staffing and Fees

[RESPONSIBLE ATTORNEY] will have primary responsibility for the matter,
assisted by associates and paralegals where that keeps fees proportionate.
Current standard hourly rates:

| Role | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Partner | $625 |
| Of Counsel | $550 |
| Senior Associate | $450 |
| Associate | $375 |
| Paralegal | $195 |

Rates are reviewed each January. Time is recorded in six-minute increments.
Invoices issue monthly and are payable within thirty days; balances more
than sixty days past due may result in suspension of work after written
notice.

## Retainer

We require an initial retainer of $[AMOUNT], to be held in our client trust
account and applied against final invoices. We may ask that the retainer be
replenished for matters in active litigation.

## Conflicts and Confidentiality

We have run our customary conflicts check and identified no current
conflict. Our duty of confidentiality continues beyond the end of the
engagement. Please read the enclosed disclosure regarding our use of
third-party research and e-discovery vendors.

## Termination

Either party may terminate the engagement on written notice, subject to
court approval where required. You remain responsible for fees and costs
incurred through the effective date of termination.

Please sign and return the enclosed copy. We are pleased to be working with
you.

Very truly yours,

Eleanor Hartwell
Managing Partner
