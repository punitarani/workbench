# Billing & Time Entry Guidelines

These are the firm's working rules for recording time and moving invoices
out the door. Questions go to the Billing Coordinator in #billing.

## Recording time

* Record time daily. Entries recorded more than five business days after
  the work are flagged on the prebill and need attorney initials.
* Six-minute increments. Round to the nearest increment, not up.
* Narratives describe the work product, not the activity in the abstract:
  "Draft and revise indemnification rider" rather than "attention to
  documents". Never name a settlement figure or strategy in a narrative —
  invoices get forwarded.
* Block billing over 2.0 hours is split into its component tasks.
* Non-billable firm work goes to the ADMIN matter, never to a client
  matter at a zero rate.

## The monthly cycle

1. **1st business day** — time cutoff for the prior month.
2. **3rd business day** — prebills circulate to responsible attorneys.
3. **8th business day** — marked-up prebills back to billing. Unreturned
   prebills escalate to the Managing Partner.
4. **10th business day** — invoices issue.
5. **Weekly** — collections review of anything over sixty days.

## Write-downs and trust

Write-downs over ten percent of a prebill require the responsible
attorney's note explaining why; over twenty-five percent requires Managing
Partner approval. Trust balances are reconciled monthly, and a matter may
not carry a negative trust balance for any reason — costs advance from
operating, not from another client's funds.
