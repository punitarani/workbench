# Records Retention & Security Policy

Raj administers systems; Owen administers retention. Questions go to
either, not to guesswork.

## Retention periods

- Client tax returns and supporting workpapers: seven years from filing.
- Audit and assurance workpapers: seven years from report date.
- Engagement letters and signed authorizations: ten years.
- Payroll processing records: six years.
- Internal administrative records: three years unless under hold.

## Storage

Client documents live in the document management system under the
engagement folder — never in personal drives, desktop folders, or email.
The portal is for transfer, not storage: items are filed to the
engagement folder the day they arrive and removed from the portal within
thirty days.

## Security

- Client data never leaves firm systems. No personal email, no personal
  cloud accounts, no USB drives.
- Screens lock at five minutes. Laptops are encrypted and must be
  physically secured outside the office.
- Suspected phishing or account compromise is reported to IT the moment
  it is noticed. Reporting a false alarm costs nothing; sitting on a
  real one costs everything.

## Destruction

Records past retention are destroyed on the quarterly cycle after the
administering manager confirms no litigation hold or open request
applies.
