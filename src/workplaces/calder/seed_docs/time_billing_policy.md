# Time & Billing Policy

Applies to every timekeeper. Owen administers the policy; exceptions
need a partner's sign-off.

## Recording time

- Enter time daily. Entries older than one week need a note explaining
  the delay.
- Record in tenths of an hour against the correct engagement. General
  firm work goes to the administrative code, never to a client.
- Narratives describe the work performed ("prepare adjusting entries and
  update lead sheets"), not the hours ("worked on file"). A reviewer
  should understand the entry without opening the workpapers.
- Mark non-billable time honestly. Write-offs happen at billing, not in
  the entry.

## Billing cycle

- Prebills circulate on the first business day of the month. Partners
  return markups within three business days.
- Invoices go out by the tenth. The office manager applies retainers
  before billing new time.
- Realization below eighty percent on any engagement gets a note in the
  billing file explaining why.

## Rates

Standard rates are set annually in the rate sheet. Discounts and fixed
fees are recorded on the engagement and honored until re-negotiated in
writing.
