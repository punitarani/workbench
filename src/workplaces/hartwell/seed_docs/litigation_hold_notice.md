# Litigation Hold Notice — Template

**To:** [CUSTODIAN LIST]
**From:** [RESPONSIBLE ATTORNEY], Hartwell & Marsh LLP
**Re:** Preservation of documents — [MATTER NAME]

You are receiving this notice because [CLIENT NAME] reasonably anticipates
litigation concerning [SUBJECT]. Until you are notified in writing that
this hold has been released, you must preserve every record described
below, in whatever form it exists.

## What to preserve

* Email, chat messages, and text messages touching on [SUBJECT], including
  messages on personal devices used for work.
* Drafts, memoranda, spreadsheets, presentations, and notes, whether
  stored locally, on shared drives, or in cloud applications.
* Calendars, call logs, and expense records for the period [DATE RANGE].
* Physical documents: do not shred, discard, or annotate originals.

## What to stop

* Suspend automatic deletion, journaling limits, and retention policies
  for the custodians and sources listed above.
* Do not delete, edit, or "clean up" any covered record, even material
  that seems irrelevant, duplicative, or embarrassing. Relevance is
  counsel's call, not the custodian's.

## Practical points

Preservation is not collection: keep working normally except as described
here, and do not forward covered material to others to "keep a copy" —
that creates new copies we must track. If a device holding covered
material is lost, damaged, or scheduled for replacement, tell us before
anything is wiped.

Acknowledge receipt by replying to this notice. Direct questions to the
responsible attorney rather than discussing the matter internally.

This notice is attorney work product. Do not forward it outside the
company.
