# Asset Purchase Agreement — Solstice Vineyards (Executed Precedent)

**Asset Purchase Agreement** dated as of September 12, 2025, between
**Solstice Vineyards, LLC**, a California limited liability company
("Buyer"), and **Halloran Cellars, Inc.**, a California corporation
("Seller").

## 1. Purchased Assets

Seller sells, and Buyer purchases, the assets listed on Schedule 1.1: the
bottling line and associated equipment, the inventory of finished and bulk
wine, the "Halloran" trademark registrations, the customer list, and the
assigned contracts on Schedule 1.1(d). Excluded assets: cash, accounts
receivable accrued before Closing, and the real property at 18 Vine Road,
which is addressed by a separate lease assignment.

## 2. Purchase Price

$2,350,000, paid as follows: $2,100,000 in cash at Closing; $250,000 held
back for eighteen months against indemnification claims (the "Holdback").
The Holdback bears no interest and is Buyer's sole source of recovery for
claims under Section 7, except for fraud and the Fundamental
Representations.

## 3. Representations of Seller

Customary representations, including: due organization; authority; no
conflicts; title to the Purchased Assets free of liens other than
Permitted Liens; material contracts listed and in force; compliance with
alcoholic beverage licensing; taxes paid; no undisclosed liabilities; and
no litigation pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened.

## 4. Covenants

Seller operates the business in the ordinary course through Closing, and
for four years will not compete in wine production within Sonoma, Napa, or
Mendocino counties, nor solicit employees or customers of the business.

## 5. Conditions to Closing

Assignment consents for the contracts marked with an asterisk on Schedule
1.1(d); ABC license transfer approval; no Material Adverse Effect.

## 6. Indemnification

Seller indemnifies Buyer for breaches of representations, capped at the
Holdback except for Fundamental Representations (capped at the Purchase
Price) and fraud (uncapped). Basket: $25,000, tipping.

*Executed copy on file; schedules maintained in the deal room folder.*
