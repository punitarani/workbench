# NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

**Vantage Data Services LLC** ("Vantage") and **Argent Systems, Inc.**
("Company").

1. **Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means any and
   all information disclosed by Vantage to Company, in any form, whether or
   not marked or identified as confidential.

2. **Obligations of Company.** Company shall hold all Confidential
   Information in strict confidence, shall not disclose it to any third
   party, and shall use it solely to evaluate the services proposed by
   Vantage. Company's obligations under this Agreement are perpetual.

3. **No obligations of Vantage.** For the avoidance of doubt, this
   Agreement imposes confidentiality obligations on Company only. Vantage
   assumes no obligations with respect to information received from
   Company.

4. **Personnel restrictions.** During the term of discussions and for two
   (2) years thereafter, Company shall not solicit for employment any
   employee of Vantage involved in the discussions.

5. **Remedies.** Company acknowledges that any breach will cause
   irreparable harm entitling Vantage to injunctive relief without bond and
   without the need to prove damages.

6. **Governing law.** State of Texas.

7. **Assignment.** Vantage may assign this Agreement freely; Company may
   not assign without Vantage's prior written consent.
