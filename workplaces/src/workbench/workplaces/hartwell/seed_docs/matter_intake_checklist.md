# Matter Intake Checklist

Every new matter goes through this checklist before any substantive work is
recorded. The paralegal on intake duty owns steps 1 through 6; the
responsible attorney signs off on 7 and 8.

1. **Conflicts check.** Search the conflicts database for the client, all
   adverse parties, and any parent or affiliate named in the request.
   Print the search results to the matter file. A hit goes to the Managing
   Partner before anything else happens.
2. **Client record.** Confirm the client organization exists in Clio with
   a current billing contact, address, and payment terms. New clients need
   a completed intake form and a W-9 on file.
3. **Open the matter.** Create the matter in Clio with the correct
   practice area, responsible attorney, and originating attorney. The
   description should say what we are actually doing, not just the party
   names.
4. **Engagement letter.** Generate from the standard form, have the
   responsible attorney review the scope paragraph, and send for
   signature. No substantive work before the signed copy is back, except
   with Managing Partner approval noted in the matter.
5. **Retainer.** Confirm the retainer amount with billing, and confirm
   receipt into trust before the first invoice cycle.
6. **Calendar.** Enter every known deadline — statutes of limitation,
   response dates, hearing dates — into the firm calendar with two
   reminders each.
7. **Staffing.** Responsible attorney confirms staffing and rate schedule.
8. **File hygiene.** Confirm the workspace exists in the document system
   under the client's folder with the standard subfolder set (Corr,
   Drafts, Executed, Research, Billing).

A matter with an unchecked box does not get time recorded against it.
Exceptions are noted in the matter with who approved them and when.
