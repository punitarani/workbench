# Records Retention Policy

Applies to every record the firm creates or receives, in any medium. The
Records Clerk administers the schedule; exceptions require the Operations
Manager's written approval.

## Retention schedule

| Record class | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Client matter files | 10 years after matter close |
| Executed agreements and instruments | Permanent |
| Trust accounting records | 5 years after final disbursement (State Bar minimum) |
| Billing records and invoices | 8 years |
| Conflicts database entries | Permanent |
| Firm financial and tax records | 7 years |
| Personnel files | 6 years after separation |
| Visitor logs and facilities records | 2 years |

## Working rules

* The official matter file lives in the document management system.
  Convenience copies — printouts, desktop folders, personal notebooks —
  are not records and are destroyed when the need ends.
* Original signed instruments (wills, deeds, undertakings) go to the fire
  safe, with a scan filed in Executed and a location note in the matter.
* On matter close, the responsible attorney certifies the file is
  complete; the Records Clerk then starts the retention clock and records
  the destruction-eligible date.
* Client property is returned at close upon request; we keep a copy at
  firm expense where the file is needed for our own defense.

## Destruction

Destruction runs twice a year from the eligible list, by shredding or
certified media destruction, and each run produces a destruction log:
matter, record class, date, method, and approver.

## Holds

A litigation hold suspends this schedule for every record it covers, no
matter the class. Nothing on the eligible list is destroyed until the
Records Clerk confirms in writing that no hold applies.
