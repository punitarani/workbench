# Commercial Lease — Pelican Bay Marina (Executed Precedent)

**Lease Agreement** dated March 1, 2021, between **Bayfront Holdings LP**
("Landlord") and **Pelican Bay Marina, Inc.** ("Tenant") for the premises
at 7 Pelican Way, Alameda, California: the harbormaster building, the
chandlery retail space, and dock storage areas B and C, approximately
11,400 square feet in total.

## Term and Renewal

Initial term of five years, expiring February 28, 2026. Tenant holds two
successive five-year renewal options, each exercisable by written notice
no later than **nine months before expiration**, at the then-current Base
Rent adjusted per Section 4.2. Failure to give timely notice extinguishes
the option — the lease is deliberate that holding over does not exercise
it.

## Rent

Base Rent of $22,800 per month, increasing three percent annually on each
anniversary. Percentage rent of two percent of chandlery gross sales above
$1.4 million per lease year, reported quarterly with an annual true-up.
Triple net: Tenant pays taxes, insurance, and common area maintenance,
with CAM reconciled annually and capped at five percent year-over-year
growth on controllable costs.

## Use and Maintenance

Permitted use: marina operations, vessel service, and marine retail.
Tenant maintains the docks and gangways; Landlord maintains the seawall,
pilings, and structural elements of the harbormaster building. Dredging of
the fairway is Landlord's obligation, required once during each five-year
term, timed to avoid the May–September peak season.

## Assignment

No assignment or sublease without Landlord's consent, not to be
unreasonably withheld; a change of control of Tenant counts as an
assignment. Landlord's consent is not required for subleases of individual
dock storage bays in the ordinary course.

## Default and Remedies

Ten-day cure for monetary default after notice; thirty days for
non-monetary defaults, extended if cure is diligently pursued. Landlord's
remedies include termination and reletting with recovery under Civil Code
Section 1951.2.
