# ADR-0004 — Procedural book, authored content

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-08-14

## Context

v1 had 11 clients and 21 matters — 10–20× under a real 17-person firm's
book (120–400 clients). Every v1 client was a full LLM actor with its own
cognition, so scaling the book to 150 clients would have scaled LM cost
with it, and most of those actors would sit idle: a real firm's book has
a long dormant tail, with only 20–40 clients in active correspondence in
any given month.

The v1 pivot directive is explicit that the simulation must be primarily
LLM-driven, so this decision needs care: it must not smuggle the old
procedural-chronicle split back in through a side door.

## Decision

Split by **kind of thing**, not by kind of day:

- **Structure is procedural**, derived from the world seed: the client
  book, engagement records, invoice cycles, ledger rows, AR aging — the
  scaffolding that in a real firm is generated by systems, not written
  by people.
- **Content is authored**: every piece of *language or judgment* — the
  correspondence, deliverables, notes, reflections, timesheet narratives
  — comes from an LM call, grounded against that structure.
- **Client actors** are minted only for clients in active correspondence
  (20–40 per month, seeded rotation), not for the whole book.

This is categorically different from the v1 chronicle split, which
procedurally fabricated *events people would have authored*. Nothing a
person would have written is procedurally generated here.

## Consequences

- 120–200 clients and 250–500 engagements at roughly constant LM cost.
- The dormant tail becomes assertable (≥25% of clients with <5 logged
  hours), which is itself a realism win — v1 had no tail at all.
- Risk: procedural structure can look too tidy (uniform invoice
  amounts, evenly spaced matters). Mitigated by drawing structural
  parameters from seeded heavy-tailed distributions and asserting their
  shape in the realism suite, same as authored surfaces.
- The boundary needs policing in review: if a "structural" generator
  ever starts emitting prose, it has crossed into authorship and must
  become an LM call.
