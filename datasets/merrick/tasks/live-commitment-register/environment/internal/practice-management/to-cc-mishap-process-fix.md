### The fix

Before sending any email with more than one external recipient, or any recipient outside the client team, **read the address line aloud before hitting send.** Not skim it — actually say the names/domains out loud. This catches autocomplete substitutions and stray CC adds that a visual scan misses.

### Why this is the fix

We looked at heavier options (send-delay pop-ups, mandatory second-reviewer on external sends, restricted autocomplete). All add friction without addressing the actual failure mode, which is skimming past a wrong or extra address rather than technical error. Reading it aloud forces the pause that a visual check doesn't.
